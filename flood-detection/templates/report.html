Tips for a good photo

  • Show the ground and the water.
  • Include a familiar object for scale.
  • Take it in good light.
  • Hold the camera steady.

Stay safe

Do not walk into floodwater to take a photo. Take it from a safe, dry place.

Before you submit

The result is an estimate, not an official warning. Always follow PAGASA, NDRRMC and LGU guidance.
