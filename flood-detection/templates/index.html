How it works

Results are estimates, not official warnings

  1. 1. Send a photo

    Take or upload a street photo and add the location.

  2. 2. The model checks it

    An image classifier labels it Flood or No Flood with a probability.

  3. 3. The agent reviews

    It compares the result with recent reports from the same area.

  4. 4. You get guidance

    See a status and what to do next. The report is saved.

Emergency? Call 911. This tool does not replace PAGASA or LGU warnings.
